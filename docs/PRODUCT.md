# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

B2B founders, sales leaders, and agency or service-business owners who need qualified sales meetings but can't run outbound themselves, because the owner is the one delivering the work. They land here skeptical: they've been burned by, or have heard about, cheap lead-gen agencies that over-promise and under-deliver. Their context is evaluation, deciding whether to trust a small, done-for-you outbound partner with their pipeline.

Their job to be done: get more qualified conversations on the calendar without hiring and managing an SDR team, building email infrastructure, or flying to trade shows.

## Product Purpose

StackLeaps is a done-for-you B2B cold-email lead-generation service. It handles list building, email infrastructure, copywriting, campaign execution, and call booking. The client's only job is to show up to the calls.

The landing page's single job: convince a skeptical buyer that a solo, technical operator can reliably book qualified meetings, then get them to book a free 30-minute strategy call (Calendly). Success = booked strategy calls, not pageviews.

The service goes wide across niches and qualifies buyers by situation, not industry. The page must read as universal B2B, not tied to any one vertical.

## Brand Personality

Three words: **sharp, warm, confident.**

- **Sharp / no-fluff.** Direct and specific. The founder is a senior software engineer who left a $250K/yr job to build this; he talks like a peer who respects your time, not a vendor reading a script. No marketing hype, no buzzwords.
- **Warm / human.** A real person you'd want to talk to. The founder's face and story carry the brand; this is one operator who cares, not a faceless agency.
- **Confident / authoritative.** Proven and in command. States what it does and what it has delivered, plainly, and trusts the reader to draw the conclusion.

## Anti-references

What this must never look or feel like:

- **Generic SaaS template.** Gradient blobs, identical three-card icon grids, the hero-metric layout every startup ships. If a visitor could say "AI made that," it has failed.
- **Cheesy lead-gen agency.** "We 10x your revenue" hype, stock handshake photos, neon, fake-urgency badges and countdown timers.
- **Corporate / soulless.** Faceless big-vendor stock-photo feel. No personality, interchangeable with any other supplier.
- **Loud / pushy / salesy.** Aggressive hard-sell energy, multiple CTAs shouting at once, manipulation. One clear action per fold instead.

## Design Principles

1. **Proof over claims.** Every assertion earns trust with a specific, verifiable number or named outcome (7 qualified introductions in 10 days), never with adjectives. This is the antidote to the cheesy-agency reflex.
2. **A face, not a logo.** The founder is the product. Lead with the person and the story; that is what a faceless competitor cannot copy.
3. **Quiet confidence.** State results and stop. One dominant idea and one clear action per fold. No urgency theater, no shouting.
4. **The site is the sample.** We sell done-for-you craft, so the page itself has to demonstrate that craft. The design is a working proof of competence.
5. **Premium through detail, not decoration.** Perceived quality comes from spacing rhythm, type hierarchy, contrast, and restraint, not from more gradients, effects, or stock imagery.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target **WCAG 2.1 AA.** Body text ≥ 4.5:1 contrast; large text ≥ 3:1.
- Fully keyboard-navigable with a visible focus state (already implemented via `:focus-visible` in `globals.css`).
- **Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.** The page uses Framer Motion entrance animations and an infinite-bounce floating card; these need a reduced-motion alternative (crossfade or static). This is currently missing and should be fixed.
