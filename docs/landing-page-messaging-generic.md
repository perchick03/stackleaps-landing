# Landing Page Messaging — StackLeaps (Generic B2B)

**Purpose:** Section-by-section content brief for the generic StackLeaps landing page. Same offer and concept as the DMC version — generalized to any B2B service business selling to other businesses. No visuals, no theme guidance here. Just structure, intent, and copy direction.

**Target audience:** B2B founders, agency owners, consultants, and service-business operators who sell to other companies and want more qualified sales meetings without doing outbound themselves.

**Voice:** Personal, from Peretz. Direct, confident, casual — like the cold emails. Not agency-speak. Words that resonate: "vetted", "qualified", "introductions", "decision-makers."

**Core proof point:** 7 qualified meetings in 10 days (Balkans DMC case study). Present as established fact from a real client. Never promise this as a guarantee on the page.

**Core offer (unchanged across verticals):**
1. We find your ideal customers.
2. We reach out on your behalf with personalized messages.
3. We book qualified meetings on your calendar.
4. You pay per qualified meeting — no retainer.
5. One client per niche — no conflicts.

## Section 2: Problem

**Purpose:** Name the pain. 3 cards. Universal B2B operator pain — not vertical-specific.

**Section label:** Sound familiar?
**Section headline:** Great Offers Don't Sell Themselves

**Pain point 1: "Waiting for inbound that never comes"**
> You have a strong offer — but the right buyers don't know you exist. You're invisible to the companies that would gladly pay you.

**Pain point 2: "Relying on referrals and hope"**
> Your best clients came from referrals or chance introductions. That's great — but it doesn't scale, and you can't control when the next one shows up.

**Pain point 3: "You run the business AND chase new business"**
> You're delivering the work, managing the team, and dealing with clients — and somehow you're also supposed to be doing sales and marketing on top of it.

---

## Section 3: Solution

**Purpose:** What we do, in 3 scannable points. Outcome-focused.

**Section headline:** We Do the Outreach. You Take the Meetings.

**Benefit 1: "We find the right buyers in your target market"**
> Vetted decision-makers at companies that match your ideal customer profile — by industry, size, role, and the kind of work you want.

**Benefit 2: "We reach out on your behalf — personalized, not spam"**
> Every message is tailored to the prospect and your offer. No templates. No mass blasts. The kind of outreach that actually gets replies.

**Benefit 3: "Qualified meetings land on your calendar"**
> We handle everything from first contact to booking. You show up to the call with a prospect who already knows what you do and wants to talk. These aren't scraped lead lists — they're real conversations with decision-makers.

---

## Section 4: Process / How It Works

**Purpose:** Build credibility by showing a real system. Timeline format, 4 steps.

**Section headline:** From Onboarding to Meetings in Weeks

**Step 1: "We learn your business"**
> 30-minute onboarding call. We understand your offer, your ideal customer, your differentiators, and the markets you want to grow in.

**Step 2: "We build your campaign"**
> We research and curate a list of decision-makers at companies that fit your ICP, write personalized outreach, and set up the infrastructure.

**Step 3: "Outreach goes live"**
> Your offer gets in front of the right people. Qualified replies come in. Meetings get booked on your calendar.

**Step 4: "We optimize what's working"**
> We track results, refine targeting, and double down on what converts. Your pipeline grows over time.

**Second CTA:** Same as hero CTA.

## Section 5: Who This Is For

**Purpose:** Qualify by situation, not deal size. Right prospects should feel "this is built for me."

**Section headline:** Is This a Fit?

**Qualifier 1:** You're a B2B service business, agency, or consultancy that wants to grow but doesn't have time for outbound.
**Qualifier 2:** You want qualified meetings with decision-makers at companies that fit your ICP.
**Qualifier 3:** You'd rather focus on delivering great work than chasing new business.

Social media gets lost. Referrals don't scale. So I built a system that puts your offer directly in front of decision-makers — and only charges you when a qualified meeting actually happens. 2. The problem: how B2B service businesses currently find clients and why it's broken (15 sec)

5. What to do next: book the call (5 sec)

## Section 7: FAQ / Objection Handling

**Q1: "How is this different from what marketing agencies offer?"**
> Most agencies run ads or post on social media. We do direct, personalized outreach to specific decision-makers at companies that match your ICP. Every conversation is with someone who could realistically become a client.

**Q2: "Does cold outreach actually work?"**
> Yes — when it's relevant and personalized. We don't send mass emails. We reach the right person at the right company with a message about their business, not a generic pitch.

**Q3: "What if my niche is too specific?"**
> That's an advantage. The more specific your offer, the easier it is to find buyers looking for exactly that. Your niche is the wedge.

**Q4: "How much of my time does this take?"**
> 30 minutes for the onboarding call. After that, we handle everything. You just show up to the meetings we book.

**Q5: "What if it doesn't work?"**
> You only pay for qualified meetings that actually happen. No retainer, no setup fee, no long-term contract. If we don't deliver, you don't pay.

**Q6: "What does it cost?"**
> Pay-per-meeting — you only pay for qualified meetings that show up on your calendar. No retainer, no minimum. We'll cover exact pricing on the strategy call based on your target market and desired volume.
